    Week 6 · Introduction to Technical Animation

    Connect motion and deformation to the asset relationships that make them work.

    Syllabus-based outline. Detailed teaching examples and exercises will be added as this week is developed.

    On this pageConnection to the courseQuestions to carry forwardPrepare with existing materialDevelopment status

    Connection to the course

    Deepen the Week 2 distinction between joint pose, surface following, and playback. Follow one deformation through its source, exported data, and destination behavior before broadening to more complex animation systems.

    Questions to carry forward

    • Does the problem appear in the joint motion or the surface following?
    • Which reference relationships and dependencies must survive the handoff?
    • How will you test the complete required motion rather than a convenient pose?

    Prepare with existing material

    Revisit the related foundation and choose one example whose assumptions or evidence you can explain. The glossary and reading library provide a starting point when a term is unfamiliar.
