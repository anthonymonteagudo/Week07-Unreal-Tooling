## Connection to the course

Return to the normal direction and UV-coordinate examples from Week 2. Start with a visible operation and its coordinate frame before extending the representation. A calculation becomes useful when its inputs and expected effect are clear.

## Questions to carry forward

- Which coordinate frame are these values expressed in?
- Is this quantity a position, a direction, or a scalar?
- What simple case would let you check the result by inspection?
