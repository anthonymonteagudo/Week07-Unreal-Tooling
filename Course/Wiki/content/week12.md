## Connection to the course

Return to the Maya-to-Unreal handoff and exhibit record. The first successful result is only one test. Source ownership, dependencies, reset behavior, and a clear explanation support the next delivery and the next collaborator.

## Questions to carry forward

- Can another person reproduce the intended starting state?
- Which assumptions must be recorded or checked after a change?
- How will you detect a regression and explain a known limitation?
