## Connection to the course

Extend the Week 2 material graph, instance, and data-role model. The deeper question is how to design logic and controls that support the intended appearance across variations without hiding important assumptions.

## Questions to carry forward

- Which controls express an artistic intention clearly?
- What inputs and coordinate conventions does the system assume?
- How will the comparison distinguish an appearance improvement from an unrelated lighting change?

## Prepare with existing material

Revisit the [related foundation](materials.html) and choose one example whose assumptions or evidence you can explain. The [glossary](glossary.html) and [reading library](resources.html) provide a starting point when a term is unfamiliar.
