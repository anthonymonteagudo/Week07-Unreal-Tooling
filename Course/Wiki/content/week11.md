## Connection to the course

Build on parameters, explicit data roles, validation, and reusable checks. A procedural result is useful when its controls produce understandable variations and its outputs can be tested against the intended use.

## Questions to carry forward

- Which inputs give the artist meaningful control?
- Can the same recorded inputs reproduce the intended output?
- What validation is needed when the system produces many assets?
