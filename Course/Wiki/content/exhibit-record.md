## Visitor explanation

- **Question:** What should the visitor predict or inspect?
- **Comparison:** What changes, and what stays fixed?
- **Observation:** What difference can the visitor see or measure?
- **Explanation:** Why does the evidence support this mechanism?
- **Explore further:** One useful concept or source link.

## Maintainer record

| Field | Your evidence |
|---|---|
| Exhibit and intended result | Name/ID, purpose, reference condition. |
| Source and engine assets | Actual paths or stable identities, dependencies, ownership. |
| Software context | DCC, engine, relevant importer or plugin version. |
| Starting state | How to reproduce the observation. |
| Hypothesis and alternative | What might explain the difference. |
| Test and change | The actual setting or data edit, including the prior value. |
| Evidence | Matching before/after views, measured values, or the relevant editor field. |
| Verification | Original failure retested and important unaffected behavior checked. |
| Reset | How to restore the intended comparison and editable state. |
| Limitations and next step | What is still uncertain or outside the test. |
| References | Specific sources supporting the mechanism or procedure. |

## A quick reader test

Give the record to someone who did not watch you work. Can they find the intended asset, reproduce the comparison, and identify the result? Revise the missing information before adding more prose.
