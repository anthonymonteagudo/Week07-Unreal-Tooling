## Connection to the course

Return to the difference between a hypothesis and evidence. Inspect the actual bottleneck, choose a change with a reason, then compare the intended quality and measured behavior under consistent conditions.

## Questions to carry forward

- Is the proposed bottleneck supported by a measurement?
- What viewing or workload conditions must remain comparable?
- Did the change preserve the visual and interaction requirements?
