## Connection to the course

Revisit the four lenses and the intended user of the tool. A revision should respond to a specific observation, failure, or usability problem. Compare the result against the original need instead of only describing implementation effort.

## Questions to carry forward

- Which feedback identifies a reproducible problem?
- What change offers the most useful improvement within the remaining constraints?
- What evidence would demonstrate that the revision helped?
