Draft sticker taxonomy

Sticker Meaning Use
LAB Directly supports museum observation, diagnosis, or repair Assign or reference during Labs 1–2
ADVANCED Valuable extension beyond the minimum Week 2 repair Instructor choice, later course, or ambitious groups
SUPPLEMENTAL Optional reference, tool, article, or exploration Self-directed follow-up

The key is embedded on the Agenda slide. Stickers are small grouped additions in the upper-right and can be renamed or deleted without changing the slide layouts.

Exhibit formation driven by the lecture

Ship first

  1. Normal-map interpretation and normals/shading — direct lecture coverage; existing Correct/Incorrect assets.
  2. RGB/sRGB and numeric texture data — direct lecture coverage; existing Correct/Incorrect assets.
  3. Material setup and assignment across Maya/Unreal — direct lecture coverage; source assets are present; author one controlled Incorrect duplicate.
  4. LOD generation and switching — direct lecture coverage and explicit team priority; source mesh is present; author one bad LOD chain.
  5. UV sampling/texel-density contract — direct lecture coverage; existing Correct/Incorrect assets.

Add only if time remains

  • Geometry integrity: good for a live Maya → Unreal repair demo.
  • Mips/compression: strong lecture fit and easy to author from a duplicated texture.
  • Skeletal animation/morph targets: displays exist, but full interaction still needs a skeletal-aware inspection path.
  • Instancing/HLOD: useful extension after the basic LOD station.

Keep advanced for now

  • Niagara: linked in the deck, but no validated project-authored Correct/Incorrect Niagara pair exists yet.
  • VAT: linked to the SideFX course; requires a Houdini export/material playback contract and should not block Week 2.

Recommended live-class rhythm

  1. Let students explore Lab 1 for 5–10 minutes before discussion.
  2. Use the LAB stickers to choose the lecture slides that frame each observed bug.
  3. Demonstrate one manual geometry or material repair from Unreal → Maya → Unreal.
  4. Run Lab 2 in cloned museum maps with five stations first.
  5. Use ADVANCED and SUPPLEMENTAL links only if pacing permits or as take-home exploration.

Packaging note
