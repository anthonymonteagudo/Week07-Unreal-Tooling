Additional historical depth

  • Slope: (y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁), undefined for a vertical line. This is useful algebra context, but not a separately required subject in the confirmed Week 5 outline.
  • Right triangles: length follows the Pythagorean theorem. Sine, cosine, and tangent are opposite/hypotenuse, adjacent/hypotenuse, and opposite/adjacent. A right isosceles triangle has 45° acute angles; general isosceles triangles do not.
  • Triangle laws: a²=b²+c²−2bc cos(A), and a/sin(A)=b/sin(B)=c/sin(C). Keep as reference for solving triangles beyond the unit-circle and dot-product uses taught live.
  • General matrix arithmetic: matching sizes for addition/subtraction; compatible inner dimensions for multiplication; transpose exchanges rows and columns. A zero matrix is not identity. An inverse, when it exists, reverses a mapping. General hand-computed inversion is further depth, while inverse use stays live.
  • Projection: distinguish local/world/view coordinates from clip coordinates, then perspective division and viewport mapping. Orthographic and perspective projection, clipping conventions, and full matrix derivations are further rendering depth. The live camera-space introduction is not removed.
  • Normals under nonuniform scale: use the appropriate inverse-transpose linear mapping and renormalize where applicable; do not apply the ordinary direction rule blindly. This is specialist depth beyond the rigid-transform examples.
  • Quaternion derivations, normalized quaternion lerp versus slerp, splines, easing, and procedural animation are deeper study after the core live rotation/interpolation introduction.
