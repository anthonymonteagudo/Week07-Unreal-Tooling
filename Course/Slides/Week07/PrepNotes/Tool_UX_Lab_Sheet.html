1 · Define the job

Who is this for, and what repetitive or difficult job does it help them do?

Write one realistic task for your partner. Give the goal and starting data; leave the route through the interface for them to discover.

Example: “This prop has a revised source mesh. Update its Unreal asset while keeping its material assignment.”

What visible evidence will count as success? What must remain unchanged?

2 · Prepare the test

☐ Use a copy or recoverable test scene.   ☐ Record the starting state.
☐ Make the required inputs available.   ☐ Explain how to stop safely.

Tester: operate the tool and say what you expect and notice.
Author: observe before explaining. Record every hint or intervention. Step in if work is at risk.

4 · Recovery and repeat use

Choose one relevant case on test data: empty selection, wrong input type, missing file, conflicting name, or a second run. Predict what should happen before trying it.

Mark each row: ✓ clear, ! needs work, or — not observed. Add evidence.
