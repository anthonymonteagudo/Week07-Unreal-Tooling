- Mock-ups/Sketches

- Why sketches?

- They are very cheap and inexpensive

- Fast iteration times

- Flesh out the designs before the implementation

- They invite conceptual feedback

- Can’t focus on nitty-gritty

- No minute details yet

- Can’t nit-pick polished design

- They engage others

- They are very easy to visualize

- Sketching process

- Block off time

- Sketch your first ideas for key parts

- What are the most interesting parts?

- What are the key points and events?

- Text heaviness

- Avoid words!

- Pictures are much easier to communicate

- Try replacing all words with symbols, images, pictures

- Sketch out alternatives
