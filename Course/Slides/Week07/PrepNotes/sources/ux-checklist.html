Artist-Facing Tool UX Checklist

[[00 - Technical Art Notes Index]]

  • Identify the user, task, and pain point.
  • Interview stakeholders and end users.
  • Define requirements, dependencies, risks, and ROI.
  • Sketch the workflow before coding.
  • Prototype the smallest useful version.
  • Test it with real users.
  • Track errors, usage, and time savings.
  • Document how to use it and who maintains it.
