Seven-minute peer-review rounds

Each tool gets one observed attempt. Keep the owner, tester and observer roles explicit.

07:00

State the task, input and success

Ready. The timer stops at the end; it does not advance automatically.

ElapsedActivity
0:00–0:45Owner states task, input and success
0:45–3:45Tester attempts; observer records evidence and help
3:45–5:15Compare expected and observed result
5:15–6:15Choose one strength and one revision or uncertainty
6:15–7:00Write the retest; reset and rotate

After round 3: four minutes for self review, then three minutes for the revision and retest. Use the peer packet. If blocked, allow one short recovery attempt and label the fallback mode.
