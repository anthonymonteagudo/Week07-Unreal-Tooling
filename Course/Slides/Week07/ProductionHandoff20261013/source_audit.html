UX, Qt, Blueprint and course references

Technical Artist Bootcamp: User Experience Design Applied to Technical Art

The closest topic match: UX applied to tools made by a technical-art team. Use to motivate considering artists throughout development.

Official indexed session description verified; tagged free content. Direct page retrieval failed on retry; full video not watched.

The System of Tools: Reducing Frustration in a Daily Workflow

Use the workflow-system framing for Maya → export → Unreal → verify. Friction can accumulate between tools as well as inside a panel.

Official session description read; free-content listing. Full video not watched; no clip timestamps verified.

Technical Artist Bootcamp: Production Values: Improving Quality, Longevity and Scalability

PDF pages 32–35 discuss familiar interface conventions, hierarchy, grouping, defaults, and tool flow. A concrete LOD Tools example supports professional relevance.

Free PDF text and speaker notes inspected; screenshot assets not extracted. Industry guidance, not a controlled study. Avoid turning fewer clicks into a universal goal.

The User Experience of Game Development Tools

Foundational game-tool UX preparation. Use as a broader resource after the directly applicable TA sessions.

Free presentation surfaced by search; session synopsis reviewed, deck not read in full.

Mapping User Stories in Agile

Use a short artist journey to connect individual tasks to the overall workflow. A story map is larger than one role–goal–benefit sentence.

Free article read. Teach only the small task-framing portion here; no Agile process prerequisite.

Accounting for UX Work with User Stories in Agile Projects

Useful instructor background for attaching usability criteria to a story.

Free article reviewed through search extract. Do not turn this into sprint-management instruction.

Paper Prototyping: Getting User Data Before You Code

Test a sketched interface before implementation. Let the participant indicate actions and have a facilitator supply the next paper state.

Free article read. Paper can reveal interpretation and flow issues; it cannot establish Unreal correctness or runtime performance.

Usability Testing 101

Use a realistic goal, observe the attempt, and record assistance. Distinguish what people do from their opinions about the design.

Free article read. Student peer reviews are formative evidence, not representative validation of a professional tool.

Parallel & Iterative Design

Useful diagrams distinguish exploring alternatives from refining successive versions.

Free article reviewed. Do not project its historical improvement percentages onto this class or Unreal tools.

Mental Models

People predict behavior using what they believe about a system. Ask what an artist expects before revealing the result.

Free article read; professional synthesis, not an Unreal experiment.

Memory Recognition and Recall in User Interfaces

Visible options, paths, and examples can reduce the need to remember syntax or hidden state.

Free guidance reviewed. Recognition does not imply showing every advanced setting simultaneously.

Parallel Prototyping Leads to Better Design Results, More Divergence, and Increased Self-Efficacy

A study of 33 participants designing web advertisements compared parallel and serial prototyping with equal time and feedback. The parallel condition performed better on the reported design outcomes. Teaching inference: compare a few layout alternatives before polishing one.

Free author manuscript; methods, results, and discussion inspected. Participants were not professional designers; task was ad design, not editor-tool usability. No numerical gain transferred to Unreal.

Beyond the five-user assumption: Benefits of increased sample sizes in usability testing

The published abstract reports substantial variation in problems found by five-person samples drawn from 60 users. Small tests can help without guaranteeing that all important problems have been found.

Free abstract only; full publisher article paywalled. Not required reading or a free full-paper recommendation.

Principles of great design

Useful chapters: agency 1:52, familiarity 6:04, simplicity 11:13. Transfer control, familiar behavior, and clear hierarchy to artist tools.

Official transcript and chapter list read; video not watched end to end. These are design recommendations, not experimental proof.

Essential Design Principles

Mental-model explanation around 29:36–32:00. Use expectations to discuss why a familiar-looking control can still surprise a user.

Official transcript excerpt verified; useful targeted segment, not a recommendation to watch the whole talk first.

Feedback · Human Interface Guidelines

Match interruption to consequence: routine status can stay near the work; significant, actionable risks can warrant interruption.

Free guidance read. Adapt the principle to Unreal conventions; do not imitate Apple styling or assume all confirmations are helpful.

Create and manage prototype flows

Connected frames let an instructor demonstrate a starting state, action, and result. The documented flow feature is available on all plans and requires edit access.

Official help read. A Figma file/account may be needed to author; no student account or installation required for this lesson.
