How to use this collection

Slide numbers refer to physical slides in Week_7_Python_in_Unreal_v13.pptx, including the cover. The left panel shows the actual current slide; the right panel is a proposed media asset, not a finished replacement slide.

Use MP4 for intentional start, stop and pause during teaching. Use originals when you specifically want looping GIFs. All exports are silent H.264, preserve source timing to within 0.04 seconds, and retain source resolution with at most one pixel of edge padding. The originals are unmodified. No interpolated frames or AI motion were added.

The source clips were assessed through chronological frame samples and metadata. This is a curated integration review, not a certification of tool compatibility. Dense editor footage needs large placement and pauses. No continuous motion files were found in the inspected Karol course-capture collection; those still captures remain useful in the earlier visual review.

Keep the listed credits in slide notes or a source footer. Publisher and author media are attributed; no general open media license was verified. A repository’s code license is not assumed to license every image. Provenance, source hashes, timings and editorial notes are in manifest.json; the placement list is available as CSV.
