LAB 2 PREPARATION ONE PER AUTHOR

Prepare one task for peer testing

Give every reviewer the same small task. A goal, safe starting data and observable success make three reviews comparable. Prepare for four minutes before the briefing.

Example: “Create a material instance from this texture set and assign it to the target cube. The protected cube must stay unchanged.” Success includes the visible result and correct scope, not just a success message.

Use prepared copies. If the tool cannot run, show recorded evidence or a concept. Label execution as untested when the reviewer cannot execute it. Do not install software during the round.
